# Enterprise Policy Guide

## Refund Policy
Customers are eligible for a full refund within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, a partial
refund of up to 50% may be granted at the discretion of the account team. Refunds must be
requested through the support portal with a valid order number. Once approved, refunds are
processed within 5–7 business days and credited back to the original payment method.

## Fee Schedule
- Standard plan: $29/month — includes up to 5 users and 10 GB storage.
- Professional plan: $79/month — up to 25 users, 100 GB storage, priority support.
- Enterprise plan: custom pricing — unlimited users, dedicated support, SLA guarantee.

Late payment fee: 1.5% per month on outstanding balances older than 30 days.
Early termination fee: 20% of remaining contract value.

## Data Retention Policy
Customer data is retained for 7 years in compliance with financial regulations.
Data deletion requests are processed within 30 business days.
Backups are stored in geographically redundant locations.

## Account Suspension Policy
Accounts are suspended after 3 consecutive missed payments. Suspended accounts retain data
for 90 days. Reinstatement requires full payment of outstanding balance plus a $50
reinstatement fee.

## Privacy and PII Policy
All personally identifiable information (PII) is encrypted at rest and in transit.
Employees may not share customer PII outside of approved internal systems.
Customer data is never sold to third parties.

## Escalation Policy
Issues unresolved after 2 business days are automatically escalated to a senior support agent.
Critical issues (data loss, security breach) are escalated immediately to the on-call team.
The SLA for critical issues is 4 hours response time.
